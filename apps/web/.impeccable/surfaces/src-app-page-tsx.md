# Welcome page (/) + sign-in / sign-up

Mode: Persuade. Audience: medical students meeting MedOS for the first time (first wave: the
owner's Year 3 cohort). Action: create an account (email + password or Google). Proof: the real
product, shown as real screenshots of real screens. Constraints: no testimonials, user counts,
endorsements or outcome stats; price is "30 days free, then €9.99/month" with no live billing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: MedOS is the study system already built for you; the page proves it by showing the
real app doing each step of the learning cycle. Refuses: abstract illustration, gradient blobs,
feature-icon card grids standing in for the product.

OWN-WORLD: category standard at Linear/Notion/Amboss craft. White and cool-grey neutrals, near-
black ink, one confident blue accent used for action and focus only; Inter at tight display
tracking; hairline borders, real product screenshots framed in a quiet window chrome; dark
theme as a deliberate second palette.

STORY: the visitor learns what MedOS is in one line, sees today's real study screen, follows one
lecture through the cycle, sees spaced review and weak spots, reads the honest price, and
creates an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: top bar (MedOS wordmark left; How it works, Pricing, Sign in, Start free
trial right). Left-aligned headline at ~64-72px over two lines, one sentence of subcopy, primary
"Start free — 30 days" button plus secondary "Sign in", one line on the price. Below/right, a
large real screenshot of the Today screen breaking the fold.

FORM: the category standard (canon), chosen by the owner after a bolder re-roll; seed:869699029ec24cbf.
Signature interaction: the learning-cycle section, where selecting a step swaps the real
screenshot for that step (keyboard-accessible tabs). Motion grammar: short opacity/translate
reveals, 150-250ms ease-out, none under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
