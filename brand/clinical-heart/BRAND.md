# Med OS Clinical Heart

The anatomical heart replaces the O in the Med OS wordmark. Its asymmetric silhouette and branching vessels are simplified from the glass-heart artwork so the symbol stays recognisable in a navigation bar or favicon.

- Use `medos-wordmark.svg` on light backgrounds and `medos-wordmark-on-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- Use `medos-mark.svg` where the full wordmark will not fit. The navy app tile and browser icons are supplied as SVG and PNG.
- `clinical-heart.webp` is the larger editorial artwork for launch and marketing surfaces.
- The original ring identity is preserved in the parent brand folder.

| Role             | Colour                          |

| Paper            | `#F4F5FB`                       |
| Action, light    | `#454FDB`                       |
| Action, dark     | `#A1ADFF`                       |

| Vessel highlight | `#E6EDFF`                       |

Instrument Sans remains the wordmark and UI type family. The welcome header heart beats continuously (a lub-dub about 70 times a minute), can be paused with the "Pause animations" button, and is still under reduced motion.
